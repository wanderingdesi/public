🗺️ A simple weekend plan

Saturday · 10:15 AM–12:15 PM

Diwali at the museum

Start with art, enjoy a performance or two, then lunch and home. Choose this or Touch-a-Truck, since their morning schedules overlap.

Saturday free alternative · 10:30–11:30 AM

Lakewood storytime

Stories, songs and play in the new library. Arrive early for a spot; no need to add another destination.

Sunday · 10–11:30 AM

Farm animals in Sunnyvale

Visit the Smile Farm at opening, follow your child’s curiosity, and leave before lunch. The farm stays open until 4 PM if an afternoon visit suits better.

Sunday indoor alternative · 11 AM–noon

Ohiya at Saratoga Library

Choose the Pow Wow demonstration instead of the farm for an indoor cultural outing.

Keep it manageable: One main outing each day is plenty. The 3 PM Bollywood session is an optional Saturday alternative, not another must-do stop.

🔎 Before you go

Tickets: Purchase museum admission and Touch-a-Truck tickets through the linked organizers. Library events and the farm visit are free. Lakewood explicitly requires no registration but has limited space; follow each other event’s latest attendance instructions.
