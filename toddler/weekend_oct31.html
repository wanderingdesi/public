🍂 SOUTH BAY · TODDLER WEEKEND · 2026

A gentle, joyful weekend for little explorers.

A practical shortlist for Saturday, October 31 through Sunday, November 1. Start with Lakewood Library’s free grand opening or choose another daytime Halloween outing, then save Sunday for colorful Día de los Muertos art, music and movement.

✨ Best bets for a 3-year-old

📚
01 · SATURDAY · FEATURED FREE PICK

Lakewood Library Grand Opening

Free · Music + crafts

Saturday, Oct 31 · 10 AM–5 PM
Lakewood Branch Library and Learning Center
760 Lakechime Drive, Sunnyvale

Explore the new children’s space, enjoy live music, watch Maker Lab demonstrations and join family activities. Costumes are encouraged, with a treat for visitors who dress up. A lovely chance for a 3-year-old to discover a new library.

Schedule:
10:15–10:20 AM · Ribbon-cutting
10:30 AM–5 PM · Family activities and demonstrations
11 AM · Alphabet Rockers performance
1 PM · Family crafts with Rough Edge Collective and Ride Art Studios
3 PM · The Sound Project performance

Planning: Free admission. The announcement lists no advance registration requirement. Suggested visit: 10:30 AM–noon for exploring and the 11 AM performance, or arrive at 1 PM for crafts.

02 · SATURDAY · SCIENCE ALTERNATIVE

Tech or Treat

Best first trick-or-treat

10 AM–5 PM · The Tech Interactive, San Jose

A not-too-scary daytime Halloween with trick-or-treat stations, hands-on science, photo spots and spooky crafts. Candy and non-food options are available.

Planning: Paid admission; general admission listed at $38 adults and $28 child/senior/student. Optional slime workshops cost extra. Choose a 60–90 minute visit and leave plenty of energy for the evening.

03 · SATURDAY · INDOOR OPTION

Halloween at the Purple Museum

Good for rainy weather

9:30 AM–4:30 PM · Children’s Discovery Museum, San Jose

Costume parade, Glow Bones, a Monster Mash Dance Zone, fall art and a Bernoulli Blower Ghost. The Wonder Cabinet also gives younger children a calmer place to reset.

Planning: Included with museum admission. Costume Spotlight is at 11 AM and 2 PM; the art activity runs 10 AM–3 PM.

Saturday–Sunday · Sunnyvale

05 · SUNDAY · TOP PICK

Día de los Muertos at the Purple Museum

Best creative outing

10 AM–3:30 PM · Children’s Discovery Museum, San Jose

A joyful cultural celebration with sand painting, calavera art, marigold butterfly masks, Aztec dancing, giant skeleton puppets and family processions.

Planning: Paid museum admission; special activities are included. Processions are listed at 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM, with dancing at 10:30 AM.

Día de los Muertos at Santana Row

Open-air + music

12–5 PM market · 377 Santana Row, San Jose

Face painting, Catrina/Catrin photo opportunities, a cultural pop-up market, mariachi and dance performances in the park.

Planning: Free community event. Face painting and photo ops are listed 12–3 PM; mariachi and dance run 1–3 PM. It is busier and less contained than the museum.

🗺️ A simple weekend plan

Saturday · 10:30 AM–noon

Lakewood Library Grand Opening

Explore the new children’s space and family activities, then catch Alphabet Rockers at 11 AM. Choose Tech or Treat instead if you prefer a museum outing.

Saturday evening

Neighborhood trick-or-treat

Keep the evening local and short: a few familiar homes, one snack, then home.

Sunday · 10:30 AM–12:30 PM

Día de los Muertos at CDM

Catch the dancing and early procession, then make art while attention is still fresh.

Sunday alternative · 1–3 PM

Santana Row

Choose this instead if you want free outdoor music, face painting and a more open-ended stroll.

My pick: Lakewood Library’s free grand opening on Saturday and the Children’s Discovery Museum Día de los Muertos celebration on Sunday. Music, books and hands-on activities make a varied weekend without needing an all-day visit.

🔎 Registration and verification notes

Tech or Treat and both Children’s Discovery Museum events require paid museum admission; buy from the official organizer pages. Lakewood Library’s opening and Santana Row are free; the Lakewood announcement lists no advance registration requirement. The Sunnyvale Autumn Food Festival was found in a current event listing but did not have a clear official schedule page in the final check, so confirm before making it the destination.

Event details checked October 31, 2026. Hours, pricing and capacity can change; confirm same-day details before leaving.
