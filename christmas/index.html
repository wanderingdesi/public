    Sunnyvale & Santa Clara

    Five picks close to home.

    🎶
    01 · Top pick

    Bluebonnet Lights

    Best synchronized light show

    955–976 Bluebonnet Dr, Sunnyvale, CA 94086

    Six neighboring homes run their lights in sync from one master show player, with music on 101.9 FM. Tune in from the car or bring a portable radio and walk the block. Winner of the 2024 Neighborhood Holiday Spirit Award from Raley's–Nob Hill Foods, and a sure hit with kids who love lights that “dance.”

    • Hours (2025): Sun–Thu 5:30–10 pm · Fri–Sat 5:30–11 pm
    • Season: Friday after Thanksgiving through the first Saturday after New Year's Day or Jan. 5, whichever comes first.
    • Note: the website was last updated March 2025, so check Facebook for 2026.

    02 · Little-kid favorite

    Arlington Court Snowpeople

    Best for young kids

    909 Arlington Ct, Sunnyvale, CA 94087

    A quiet cul-de-sac yard full of glowing snowpeople, elves and other inflatables, plus a nativity scene. The snow machine makes it a favorite photo stop.

    • Hours: from 5:30 pm nightly
    • Season (2025): Nov. 29 – Jan. 3

    03 · Walking street

    Heritage District Holiday Arches

    Stroller-friendly stroll

    E. McKinley Ave (Bayview to Central), Sunnyvale, CA 94086

    Glowing light arches line the sidewalks. It began in 2020 as “Arches of Hope on McKinley” with 25 houses and has spread to nearby Heritage District streets. Pairs well with downtown Murphy Avenue.

    • Hours: not published (residents' own timers), so aim for about 5:30–9 pm
    • Season (2025): fullest from mid-December

    Capitola Way

    Santa Clara “Best of the Best”

    954 & 960 Capitola Way, Santa Clara, CA 95051

    Top citywide prize in Santa Clara's 2025 contest. In 2023, 960 had a fully lit yard and a giant mailbox where kids could send letters to Santa.

    • Hours & dates: not published. Contest homes are usually lit evenings from early December to New Year's.

    Drive times are approximate, from Lawrence Expressway & El Camino Real with no traffic.

    🐧
    06 · About 10 min

    Lamore Lights

    Closest San Jose show

    1419 Lamore Dr, San Jose, CA 95130 West San Jose, near Hathaway Park

    A lighted arch spans the street, and the yard is full of glowing reindeer, snowpeople and hockey-playing penguins under a sparkling rooftop star. The lights flash to music like Trans-Siberian Orchestra's “Christmas in Sarajevo”; past years ran two shows every 15 minutes.

    • Hours: 6:30–9 pm
    • Season (2025): Nov. 30 – Dec. 31
    • Note: one older guide gave the radio station as 89.1 FM, so confirm it on the display's site.
    Get directions ↗Display details ↗
    🍭
    07 · About 20 min

    Willow Glen Holiday Streets

    Best neighborhood walk

    Start at the 1000 block of Glen Brook Ave, San Jose, CA 95125 Also Glen Echo, Glen Eyrie and Cherry avenues, plus Camino Ramon, Camino Pablo and Camino Ricardo

    Whole streets go in on a shared theme, with light archways, lit poles, candy canes, giant snowpeople, and Santa with his reindeer. Walking it is free and makes a full evening, but parking gets tight on weekends, so arrive around sunset.

    • Hours: not official. Most homes are decorated by the first weekend of December and lit in the evening.
    • Optional paid extra (2025): Willow Glen Business Association trolley rides, Dec. 7–18, 5:15–9 pm, $20–$40
    Get directions ↗Street-by-street guide ↗Mercury News ↗

    08 · About 20 min

    Dave's Christmas Lights

    Meet Santa, for a good cause

    1851 Cherry Ave, San Jose, CA 95125 Willow Glen

    A longtime Willow Glen display that raises money for the Make-A-Wish Foundation. On special nights Santa and Mrs. Claus are there for photos. An easy add-on to a Willow Glen walk.

    • Hours (2025): 6:30–9 pm daily in December. Check Facebook for Santa nights.
    • Note: an older report said the lights come down Dec. 26, so go before Christmas.
    Get directions ↗Display details ↗NBC Bay Area ↗

    09 · About 15–20 min

    Canopy of Lights

    Most dramatic light canopy

    1026 Houghton Ct, San Jose, CA 95112

    Strands of LED lights hang from about 30 feet up and shift from bright white to colors and patterns over a sea of other lights, synced to music on 92.9 FM.

    • Hours (2025): starts at 6 or 7 pm depending on the day, until 10 pm
    • Season (2025): Dec. 1 – Jan. 4
    • Note: these details come from a listing's search preview, so confirm them first.

    10 · About 25 min

    Christmas in San Jose!

    Biggest music-synced show

    1473 Glacier Dr, San Jose, CA 95118 Almaden area, the farthest pick

    About 70,000 lights synced to music on 92.9 FM or the PulseMesh app (code GX42). Decorating since 2008, the homeowner collects donations for the American Cancer Society's Relay For Life.

    • Hours (2025): 5–10:30 pm
    • Season: listed for Nov. 23, 2026 – Jan. 12, 2027 (Dec. 1 – Jan. 3 in 2025)

    🗺️ An easy evening plan

    Two stops, about 90 minutes. Pick one.

    Option A · Sunnyvale

    Best with young kids

    5:30 pm
    Arlington Court SnowpeopleGo early while little ones are fresh: inflatables, snow and photos.
    ~6:30 pm
    Bluebonnet LightsTune to 101.9 FM and watch a few songs from the car, or walk the block.
    Bonus
    McKinley Avenue archesStill have energy? Finish with a short walk near downtown.

    Option B · Santa Clara winners

    Best after the late-December announcement

    Stop 1
    Capitola Way (954 & 960)See the Best of the Best homes and look for the Santa mailbox.
    Stop 2
    E. River Parkway (967–1021)Park once and walk the Best Group row.

    Option C · San Jose

    A music show, then a Willow Glen walk

    6:30 pm
    Lamore LightsAbout 10 minutes down Lawrence. Catch a couple of the music shows.
    ~7:15 pm
    Willow GlenAbout 15 minutes farther. Walk the themed streets and finish at Dave's Christmas Lights (1851 Cherry Ave) before 9 pm.
    Please be considerate: These are private homes. Stay on public sidewalks, keep off lawns, park legally, keep voices and engines low, and never block driveways. This guide can't confirm that a display is lit tonight.
