Before you begin

When and how to use this guide

Lakshmi Puja is done on Kartik Amavasya, in the evening. In 2026 that falls on Sunday, 8 November. Amavasya runs from 11:27 AM on Sunday to 12:31 PM on Monday in New Delhi. The guide gives the Vrishabha kaal within pradosh, the hours just after sunset, as the preferred time.

How this guide is made

The order and mantras follow the Gita Press Nitya Karma Puja Prakash (NKPP), Shri Mahalakshmi-pujan, pp. 259–276. The full vidhi includes prana-pratishtha, the navagraha, the kalash, and puja of the ledgers. This guide keeps twelve steps a home can do on its own. The book allows worship yathalabdhopachara, with whatever is at hand.

If a mantra won’t come, say its meaning in your own words. In every Sanatan practice, bhaav comes first. If you can, do the full vidhi with a pandit. Where your family does it differently, keep your family’s way.

सामग्री · Samagri

Keep these ready before sunset

Setting up

चौकी
  • A chowki or low table and a clean red or yellow cloth
  • Ganeshji’s and Maa Lakshmi’s murtis or pictures
  • A clean plate and coins or money (द्रव्य-लक्ष्मी)
  • Chandan with kesar, or kumkum, for the lotus

Asan and purifying

आसन · आचमन
  • An asan of wool, kusha, or cloth
  • A lota of clean water
  • A small spoon (achamani)
  • Gangajal, if you have it

For the sankalp and Ganeshji

संकल्प · गणेश
  • Akshat (whole rice)
  • Roli or kumkum for tilak
  • Flowers and durva grass
  • A laddoo or sweet

For Maa Lakshmi

स्नान · श्रृंगार
  • Panchamrit made with milk, curd, ghee, honey, and sugar
  • Clean water for her bath
  • Chandan, kumkum, and akshat coloured with kumkum
  • Red flowers or a lotus, and a flower garland

Dhoop, deep, and bhog

धूप · दीप · नैवेद्य
  • Dhoop or agarbatti, the main diya with ghee, cotton wicks, and a matchbox
  • Mithai and seasonal fruit
  • Paan leaves, supari, and cardamom
  • A coin for dakshina, kheel, and batashe

Diyas and aarti

दीपमालिका · आरती
  • 11 or 21 diyas, oil or ghee, and wicks
  • An aarti thali, a ghee wick, and camphor
  • A bell
  • Sugarcane, oranges, and water chestnuts, by family custom

The puja · Steps 1 to 3

Set the place, purify, and take the sankalp

1

The chowki

चौकी

Before sunset, clean the house and puja place, and bathe. Spread a clean cloth on the chowki. Place Ganeshji and, on his right, Maa Lakshmi. As you face them, she is on your left.

Beside her, on a clean plate, draw an eight-petal lotus in saffron chandan or kumkum and place your coins on it. The money is worshipped with her.

NKPP p. 259 gives Maa Lakshmi’s position and specifies saffron chandan for the lotus.

2

Asan, achaman, and sprinkling

आसन · आचमन · प्रोक्षण
  1. Sit on your own asan, facing east or north. Purify the asan.

    ॐ पृथ्वि त्वया धृता लोका देवि त्वं विष्णुना धृता ।
    त्वं च धारय मां देवि पवित्रं कुरु चासनम् ॥

    Om prithvi tvaya dhrita loka devi tvam vishnuna dhrita. Tvam cha dharaya mam devi pavitram kuru chasanam.

    Meaning. O Earth, you hold up the worlds, and Vishnu holds you. Hold me too, Devi, and make this seat pure.

  2. Achaman. Hold your right hand in gokarna mudra, like a cow’s ear, with a few drops of water. Sip three times from the base of the thumb, one name each.

    ॐ केशवाय नमः । ॐ नारायणाय नमः । ॐ माधवाय नमः ।

    Om keshavaya namah. Om narayanaya namah. Om madhavaya namah.

    Meaning. Salutations to Keshava, to Narayana, to Madhava.

    Wipe the lips and wash the hand. ॐ हृषीकेशाय नमः । ॐ गोविन्दाय नमः ।

    Some homes say स्वाहा in place of नमः. In the south, अच्युताय · अनन्ताय · गोविन्दाय. Keep your family’s way.

  3. Sprinkle a little water on yourself and the puja things.

    अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
    यः स्मरेत्पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥

    Apavitrah pavitro va sarvavastham gato’pi va. Yah smaret pundarikaksham sa bahyabhyantarah shuchih.

    Meaning. Pure or impure, in whatever state, whoever remembers the lotus-eyed Lord is pure, inside and out.

NKPP describes this opening for every puja. The sprinkling verse is from the Garuda Purana. When water is unavailable, touching the right ear counts as achaman (Devi Bhagavata 11.3).

3

Sankalp

संकल्प

Take a little water, a few grains of akshat, and a flower in your right hand. Say your name, your gotra, your city, and that today, on Kartik Amavasya, you do Maa Lakshmi’s puja. Then let it fall near Ganeshji. Your puja has begun.

Put your city, gotra, and name where the Gita Press sankalp says अमुक (amuka). You can also say it in your own words.

ॐ विष्णवे नमः, ॐ विष्णवे नमः, ॐ विष्णवे नमः। ॐ अद्य ब्रह्मणोऽह्नि द्वितीयपरार्धे श्रीश्वेतवाराहकल्पे वैवस्वतमन्वन्तरेऽष्टाविंशतितमे कलियुगे कलिप्रथमचरणे बौद्धावतारे भूलोके जम्बूद्वीपे भरतखण्डे भारतवर्षे अमुकनगरे रौद्रनामसंवत्सरे मासोत्तमे मासे कार्तिकमासे कृष्णपक्षे पुण्यायाममावास्यायां तिथौ रविवासरे अमुकगोत्रोत्पन्नः अमुकनामाऽहं श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणोक्तफलावाप्तिकामनया ज्ञाताज्ञातकायिकवाचिकमानसिकसकलपापनिवृत्तिपूर्वकं स्थिरलक्ष्मीप्राप्तये श्रीमहालक्ष्मीप्रीत्यर्थं महालक्ष्मीपूजनं कुबेरादीनां च पूजनं करिष्ये। तदङ्गत्वेन गौरीगणपत्यादिपूजनं च करिष्ये।

Meaning. Om, salutations to Vishnu (three times). Today, in this day of Brahma, in the second half of his life, in the Shveta Varaha kalpa, in the 28th age of Vaivasvata Manu, in the first quarter of the Kali Yuga, in the age of the Buddha avatar, on this earth, in Jambudvipa, in Bharat, in the city of ___, in the year named Raudra, in Kartika, the best of months, in the dark fortnight, on the holy Amavasya, on Sunday, I, ___, born in the ___ gotra, seeking the fruit the Shruti, Smriti, and Puranas speak of, and first setting down every sin, known and unknown, of body, speech, and mind, for Lakshmi who stays, and to please Shri Mahalakshmi, will do the puja of Mahalakshmi and of Kubera and the others. As part of it, I will also do the puja of Gauri, Ganapati, and the others.

NKPP pp. 35 and 260. Raudra is this year’s samvatsara (Vikram Samvat 2083). रविवासरे means Sunday. Women say गोत्रोत्पन्ना (gotrotpanna).

The puja · Steps 4 to 9

Ganeshji first, then Maa Lakshmi’s offerings

4

Ganeshji first

गणेश पूजन

Every puja begins with Ganeshji. Offer him tilak, akshat, a flower, durva, and a laddoo.

ॐ गं गणपतये नमः ।

Om gam ganapataye namah.

Meaning. Om, salutations to Ganapati.

Words for the offerings in steps 5 to 9. Say ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै नमः, then name what you offer and say समर्पयामि (“I offer”).

Om mahalakshmyai namah, pushpam samarpayami. “Om, salutations to Mahalakshmi. I offer this flower.”

5

Call Maa Lakshmi

आवाहन · आसन

Hold a flower in folded hands and call her. Then place the flower before her as her seat.

ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै नमः । महालक्ष्मीम् आवाहयामि ॥

Om mahalakshmyai namah. Mahalakshmim avahayami.

Meaning. Om, salutations to Mahalakshmi. I call you, Mahalakshmi, to be here.

6

Her bath

स्नान

Bathe the coins on the lotus with a little panchamrit, then with clean water. The Gita Press gives the bath to the coins (द्रव्य-लक्ष्मी) to protect a clay murti. With a picture, sprinkle a few drops.

7

Adorn her

श्रृंगार

Offer chandan with the ring finger, then kumkum, akshat coloured with kumkum, red flowers or a lotus, and a garland.

8

Incense, then the diya

धूप · दीप

Show the incense, then the lit ghee diya. Wash your hands.

9

Bhog

नैवेद्य

Offer a sweet with a little water, seasonal fruit, paan with supari and cardamom, and a coin as dakshina.

Steps 4–9 follow NKPP pp. 261–270. The Gita Press notes that by family custom in some regions, Maa Lakshmi is offered haldi or dhania in place of akshat, with soaked gud as prasad.

The puja · Steps 10 to 12

The diyas, the aarti, and forgiveness

10

The diyas

दीपमालिका

Light 11, 21, or more diyas in a plate and set them near Maa Lakshmi. Offer them chandan and a flower, saying ॐ दीपावल्यै नमः।

त्वं ज्योतिस्त्वं रविश्चन्द्रो विद्युदग्निश्च तारकाः ।
सर्वेषां ज्योतिषां ज्योतिर्दीपावल्यै नमो नमः ॥

Tvam jyotis tvam ravish chandro vidyud agnish cha tarakah. Sarvesham jyotisham jyotir deepavalyai namo namah.

Meaning. You are light, the sun, the moon, lightning, fire, and the stars. Light of all lights, salutations again and again to the row of lamps.

Offer kheel to Ganeshji, Maa Lakshmi, and every deity. Offer sugarcane, oranges, or water chestnuts if your family does. Then light every diya in the house.

11

Aarti

आरती
  1. Draw a swastika on the thali. Set a ghee wick on a little akshat and flowers, with camphor beside it. Light it and sprinkle the thali with water.
  2. The whole family stands together, rings the bell, and sings Om Jai Lakshmi Mata.
  3. Move the thali 4 times at her feet, 2 at the navel, 1 at the face, and 7 around her whole form.
  4. Circle a little water around the thali. Offer the aarti to Maa Lakshmi first, then everyone takes it with both palms.

चक्षुर्दं सर्वलोकानां तिमिरस्य निवारणम् ।
आर्तिक्यं कल्पितं भक्त्या गृहाण परमेश्वरि ॥

Chakshurdam sarvalokanam timirasya nivaranam. Artikyam kalpitam bhaktya grihana parameshvari.

Meaning. You who give sight to every world and drive away the dark, accept this aarti, offered with devotion, Supreme Goddess.

The nirajan mantra and family aarti are in NKPP around p. 273. The 4–2–1–7 count is given around p. 255.

Om Jai Lakshmi Mata

ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता

Repeat the first line after every verse. Sing your family’s version if it differs.

  1. ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता, मैया जय लक्ष्मी माता ।
    तुमको निशदिन सेवत, हर विष्णु विधाता ॥

    Om jai Lakshmi mata, maiya jai Lakshmi mata. Tumko nishdin sevat, Har Vishnu Vidhata.

  2. उमा रमा ब्रह्माणी, तुम ही जग-माता ।
    सूर्य-चन्द्रमा ध्यावत, नारद ऋषि गाता ॥

    Uma Rama Brahmani, tum hi jag-mata. Surya-chandrama dhyavat, Narad rishi gata.

  3. दुर्गा रूप निरंजनि, सुख-सम्पत्ति दाता ।
    जो कोई तुमको ध्यावत, ऋद्धि-सिद्धि धन पाता ॥

    Durga roop niranjani, sukh-sampatti data. Jo koi tumko dhyavat, riddhi-siddhi dhan pata.

  4. तुम पाताल-निवासिनि, तुम ही शुभदाता ।
    कर्म-प्रभाव-प्रकाशिनि, भवनिधि की त्राता ॥

    Tum patal-nivasini, tum hi shubhdata. Karm-prabhav-prakashini, bhavnidhi ki trata.

  5. जिस घर में तुम रहतीं, सब सद्गुण आता ।
    सब सम्भव हो जाता, मन नहीं घबराता ॥

    Jis ghar mein tum rahtin, sab sadgun aata. Sab sambhav ho jata, man nahin ghabrata.

  6. तुम बिन यज्ञ न होते, वस्त्र न कोई पाता ।
    खान-पान का वैभव, सब तुमसे आता ॥

    Tum bin yagya na hote, vastra na koi pata. Khan-paan ka vaibhav, sab tumse aata.

  7. शुभ-गुण-मन्दिर सुन्दर, क्षीरोदधि-जाता ।
    रत्न चतुर्दश तुम बिन, कोई नहीं पाता ॥

    Shubh-gun-mandir sundar, kshirodadhi-jata. Ratna chaturdash tum bin, koi nahin pata.

  8. महालक्ष्मीजी की आरती, जो कोई नर गाता ।
    उर आनन्द समाता, पाप उतर जाता ॥

    Mahalakshmiji ki aarti, jo koi nar gata. Ur anand samata, paap utar jata.

12

Flowers and forgiveness

पुष्पांजलि · क्षमा-प्रार्थना

Hold flowers in both hands. Offer them, then do a pradakshina and a full pranam.

ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै नमः । मन्त्रपुष्पाञ्जलिं समर्पयामि ॥

Om mahalakshmyai namah. Mantrapushpanjalim samarpayami.

Meaning. Om, salutations to Mahalakshmi. I offer these flowers, with the mantra.

Fold your hands and ask forgiveness.

आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वरि ॥
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं सुरेश्वरि ।
यत्पूजितं मया देवि परिपूर्णं तदस्तु मे ॥

Avahanam na janami na janami visarjanam. Pujam chaiva na janami kshamasva parameshvari. Mantrahinam kriyahinam bhaktihinam sureshvari. Yat pujitam maya devi paripurnam tad astu me.

Meaning. I do not know how to call you, nor how to send you off, nor how to worship you. Forgive me, Supreme Goddess. Whatever I offered, short of mantra, of rite, of devotion, O Queen of the gods, let it be made whole.

To close

Prasad, and they stay

Pray in the words the Gita Press gives.

त्वमेव माता च पिता त्वमेव ।
त्वमेव बन्धुश्च सखा त्वमेव ।
त्वमेव विद्या द्रविणं त्वमेव ।
त्वमेव सर्वं मम देवदेव ॥

Tvameva mata cha pita tvameva, tvameva bandhush cha sakha tvameva, tvameva vidya dravinam tvameva, tvameva sarvam mama devadeva.

Meaning. You alone are my mother and my father, my kin and my friend, my knowledge and my wealth. You are everything to me, God of gods.

Then let a little water fall, saying the following.

ॐ अनेन यथाशक्त्यर्चनेन श्रीमहालक्ष्मीः प्रसीदतु ।

Om anena yathashaktyarchanena shri mahalakshmih prasidatu.

Meaning. By this worship, done as well as I could, may Shri Mahalakshmi be pleased.

Bow to the elders. Share charanamrit and prasad.

Maa Lakshmi and Ganeshji stay. NKPP gives visarjan to the other invoked deities and keeps the new Ganesh and Lakshmi in place.

The full vidhi

Prana-pratishtha, the navagraha, the kalash, the full bath with five substances, and the puja of the doorstep, the ledgers, and the tijori are part of the full vidhi. This guide covers the steps a home can do with bhaav.

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥

Patram pushpam phalam toyam yo me bhaktya prayacchati, tad aham bhaktyupahritam ashnami prayatatmanah. (Bhagavad Gita 9.26)

Whoever offers Me a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or a little water with bhakti, I accept that offering of love.
