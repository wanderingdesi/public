A few questions before you go

Is every street continuously decorated?

No. This is a selection of reported yards and neighborhood viewing points. Decorations and participation vary, and some addresses are a separate drive away.

Are these displays suitable for younger children?

Use the themes to choose: pumpkins and lights may be a gentler starting point than clowns, giant skeletons or the IT-inspired yard. Sound, motion and the current setup can change how a display feels.

Do I need tickets?

No ticket requirement was identified in the linked residential listings. That does not imply entry to anyone’s property: view from the sidewalk unless the homeowner clearly invites visitors onto a route.

Can I take photos?

Follow any homeowner signs, keep paths clear and avoid flash where it disrupts the display. Ask before photographing people closely and avoid capturing private interiors.

Are the photos from Halloween 2026?

Not necessarily. The KTVU and Chronicle features are from 2024, and the tour roundup explicitly draws on past visits. They show the character and history of these displays, not their live condition tonight.
