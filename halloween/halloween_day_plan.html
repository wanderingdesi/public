01 · MY PICK FOR ELABORATE DISPLAYS

Willow Glen · San Jose

Best mix of themed yards

Cherry Avenue · Vernon Avenue · Willow Street at Carolyn Avenue

Use Cherry and Vernon as display stops, then explore nearby residential blocks. Reported highlights include 1160 Cherry Avenue (clown-mouth house), 1183 Cherry Avenue (Addams Family) and 1305 Vernon Avenue (inflatables).

These are several stops across the neighborhood, not one continuously decorated block. The address roundup reflects past visits; individual displays may change.

🗺️ My suggested Halloween evening

Choose Willow Glen or Los Gatos as the main outing. Moving between both takes time away from exploring.

5:30 pm · Suggested arrival

Park near Willow Glen

Arrive before the busiest period. Find legal parking outside the busiest blocks and check the walking distance to your first display.

6–8 pm · Main outing

Cherry / Vernon area

Visit the themed yards, explore neighboring blocks and leave time for trick-or-treating. Treat these times as an itinerary, not official display hours.

After 8 pm · Optional

Frobisher Way, or head home

Check live traffic and everyone’s energy before adding another drive. Frobisher has scarier themes, so choose according to your group.

Prefer a whole-neighborhood outing? Use the same early-arrival approach for Tait Avenue in Los Gatos and spend the evening in Almond Grove instead.

Haunted Hollywood · Eerie Elegance

1194 Magnolia Avenue, San Jose

This year’s theme is Haunted Hollywood. The Harry Potter theme shown in older coverage was from 2025.

🔎 Before you leave

Bring comfortable shoes, a flashlight and a layer for the evening. Stay on public sidewalks unless invited onto a display’s visitor route, keep driveways clear and respect closed streets.
